## Exam Room Reservation (2026-11-10, not released yet)

- **What:** page `/exam-rooms` ("Exam Room Reservation", `ExamRoomReservationPage.tsx`). A person sees their own subjects for the Current Semester, picks a room and plots exam blocks on a date-by-time grid (drag a subject onto a cell, or pick it and click a time; `@dnd-kit` like the Scheduler). Everyone sees every block and who holds it. Two rounds per term: Midterm and Final.
- **Rounds:** `ExamPeriod` (dates + `isOpen`) per college, term and kind. The college secretary (or the admin for a college) sets the dates and **opens** the round; faculty can plot only while it is open. Opening notifies the college's teaching roles. Dates cannot shrink under existing blocks; a round with blocks cannot be deleted.
- **Who plots:** FACULTY, CHAIRMAN, DEAN, COLLEGE_SECRETARY (they teach), only for subject + class pairs they have a faculty load for in that term. The admin views and can remove blocks.
- **Rules (first plotted, first served):** a room cannot overlap another block on the same date (409 names who holds it); a student class cannot have two exams at once (409); **one person may hold several rooms at the same time** and there is **no capacity warning** (both by the user's decision: a proctor can be asked for). Times 6:00-22:00; the date must be inside the round. Plotting takes a Postgres advisory lock on the room, so two people dropping at once cannot both win.
- **Removing:** the owner removes their own block. The chairman of the **room's department** can remove any block in it by force (the owner is notified), or **ask the owner** (`ExamRemovalRequest`): the owner accepts (block removed) or declines (the chairman sees "declined" and can clear it). One pending request per block.
- **Where:** backend `modules/examRooms/{examRoomRoutes,examRoomService,examRoomSchemas}.ts` mounted at `/exam-rooms`; tables `ExamPeriod`, `ExamReservation`, `ExamRemovalRequest` (migration `20260624000000_exam_room_reservation`, college-scoped in `collegeScope.ts`). Notifications use type `EXAM_ROOM` (`/exam-rooms` link).
- **Menu:** FACULTY menu is now "Management" (Dashboard, Exam Room Reservation) and "Schedules" (Reports, Class schedule, Room availability). Other roles keep "Scheduling" (Exam Room Reservation sits after Dashboard) and "Reports". For a faculty member the enabled experiments (Grades, Portal Insert Grade) also sit in Management, each with an "Experimental" badge under its name, and the separate Experimental section is not shown to faculty (other roles still get it). Code: `facultyGroups` and the `experiments` rows in `Sidebar.tsx`. The faculty Dashboard page still shows its old "Schedules" / "Reports" card sections.
- **Tests:** `examRooms.spec.ts` (all the rules over the API), `examRoomsPage.spec.ts` (click, drag, chairman request, owner accepts, force removal), `facultyMenu.spec.ts`.
- **Not built:** per-room occupancy overview across all rooms at once, exam blocks in the PDF reports, faculty-to-room-chairman requests, an admin screen to edit blocks.
