Semester lock
 - the admin, dean and college secretary lock a semester of an academic year (Academic years page, one button per semester). While locked, nobody changes its schedule: blocks, auto-assign, reset, copy, GE import, cross-department requests, deloading and room openings are refused; the scheduler shows a banner and disables its actions. Chairmen and faculty are told in the bell. done (v0.5.0, needs migration 20260612000000_term_lock)

Audit log
 - append-only history of who changed what, for the admin, dean and college secretary only: academic years (create, rename, activate, current term, trash, restore, delete, semester lock), curriculum (and imports), subjects, student classes (create and delete), departments, rooms (and open/close), users (role, status, department, max units), scheduling rules, GE sheets, deloading, and schedule blocks added or removed (also reset, auto-assign, copy, GE import as one entry each). Not logged: schedule edits and moves, logins, anything faculty do. Per-page History button, an Audit log page with filters and CSV export. Entries copy the person's name, email and role at the time, so later role changes do not alter them. done (v0.5.0, needs migration 20260613000000_audit_log)

University and colleges
 - University > College > Department. Everything that exists sits under the College of Engineering. Each college sees only its own departments, people, rooms, curricula, subjects, classes, schedules, requests, GE sheet and rule sets; the admin creates colleges and assigns people and departments; semester locks are per college; the audit log is per college; the President role sees the whole university, read only, with a University overview. done (v0.6.0, needs migrations 20260614000000_university_college, 20260614000001_college_optional and 20260615000000_college_current_term)
 - next (v0.7.0): room sharing. When a college locks a semester, all of its rooms open to other colleges for the free slots, with approval from the host's dean, college secretary and the room's chairman. The other college's chairmen draft the schedule; their college secretary collates the proposed blocks (Requests page or a new page) and submits the request; the approvers see the newly added blocks in one scrollable page preview. Unlocking a host semester stays blocked while guest bookings exist. Academic years are shared and created by the admin or a college secretary, each name only once; the current term is per college (decided).
